Jorge Bernal Bernabé holds an MSc, Master’s, and PhD in Computer Science, as well as an MBA from the University of Murcia (Spain). He was accredited as a Full Professor by ANECA in 2020 and received the Best Doctoral Thesis Award from the School of Computer Science in 2015.

In 2017, he was awarded a postdoctoral research fellowship funded by INCIBE, and in 2019, an AXA Research Fund postdoctoral fellowship. Since February 2023, he has been a Full Professor in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering.

He has also been a visiting researcher at Hewlett-Packard Laboratories (Bristol, UK) and at the University of the West of Scotland.

He is the author of more than 45 articles in journals indexed in the Journal Citation Reports (JCR), as well as numerous conference papers and book chapters.
