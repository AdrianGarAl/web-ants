SUMMARY OF THE PROJECT

In recent years, the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become widespread, leading to a technological paradigm shift. Addressing key objectives of the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 2030 requires the responsible use of AI to convert data into actionable knowledge for societal benefit.

This trend is propelled by increasing hyperconnectivity through distributed systems integrated into Internet infrastructure, particularly leveraging Internet of Things (IoT) and 5G/6G technologies. These ecosystems enable new services for sustainable cities, communities, and advanced eHealth scenarios.

REMINDER aims to design a decentralized and secure approach for accessing and processing data from distributed systems, including an edge-based architecture for Federated Learning (FL). This architecture supports collaborative model creation without sharing raw data, addressing major security and privacy challenges associated with decentralized Machine Learning (ML) approaches. The project also focuses on GDPR compliance, cyberattack resilience, and feasibility through eHealth and smart-building use cases.

PROJECT GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

REMINDER focuses on addressing key security and privacy challenges in decentralized and distributed systems. Through the integration of advanced technologies such as Federated Learning (FL) and cryptographic approaches, REMINDER aims to develop secure and privacy-respecting methods for managing the data lifecycle.

1. Development of Advanced Authentication Protocols

REMINDER is designing new authentication protocols to ensure exclusive participation of legitimate systems during decentralized training processes.

2. Practical Applications in Health and Energy Efficiency

The project will explore advanced use cases aligned with the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), focusing on the application of FL in eHealth and energy efficiency.

3. Design of Protocols for Integration of Auxiliary Devices

REMINDER will propose an edge-enabled architecture where edge nodes will act as auxiliary components to enable the integration of constrained devices into the architecture.

4. Development of Self-Aware Systems to Identify and Adapt to New Threat Vectors

The project will design a client selection approach to identify the most suitable nodes to participate in the FL training process, considering the security level provided by different devices.

5. Creation of User-Friendly Federated Learning Techniques

REMINDER will design a privacy-preserving framework for using FL in distributed systems.

REMINDER aims to contribute to building a safer and more privacy-respecting digital future by addressing critical challenges in data management in decentralized and distributed environments.

SOCIAL IMPACT OVERVIEW

EMPOWERING DIGITAL INCLUSION

REMINDER aims to empower individuals and communities by bridging the gap to access and understand technology. Through user-friendly solutions, the project fosters digital literacy and ensures everyone can navigate the digital landscape with confidence. By prioritizing inclusivity, REMINDER contributes to a more equitable and informed society.

IMPACT ON SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

REMINDER enhances the security and privacy of decentralized and distributed systems' data lifecycle. By integrating cybersecurity and AI concepts, the project develops tools to protect sensitive information, benefiting various domains such as cyber-threat information sharing, biometric authentication, and autonomous driving.

REDUCING SOCIAL BARRIERS

REMINDER's prototypes empower users to safeguard their information, addressing the technological gap among citizens. By providing accessible solutions, the project ensures that everyone can benefit from technological advancements, regardless of background or expertise. Additionally, REMINDER aligns with legal instruments like the EU Cyber Resilience Act to support its enforcement and address societal challenges.

INTRODUCING CONSORTIUM PARTNERS

REMINDER’s consortium comprises four esteemed partners from diverse sectors across four countries: Spain, UK, Romania, and Austria. Each partner brings unique expertise and experience, collectively forming a purposeful and multidisciplinary team. With a solid track record in research and innovation, these partners embody a commitment to advancing security and privacy in decentralized systems.

REMINDER project adheres to rigorous ethical standards and guidelines outlined by Horizon EU, the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the EU, and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It emphasizes capacity building rather than exploitation of human resources and ensures that ethical considerations are paramount throughout all project activities. Personal data processing complies with GDPR regulations, with explicit informed consent obtained from participants. The project adopts Explainable AI techniques to enhance transparency and stakeholder acceptability. REMINDER maintains gender neutrality and aligns with EU regulations on gender equality, ensuring equal access to all project processes.
