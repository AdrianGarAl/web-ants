The ANTS Research Group is part of the Intelligent Systems Group at UMU , where we work on intelligent IT services and applications across a wide range of network environments (IPv4/IPv6, ad hoc, sensor, GPRS, UMTS, and others) . Our research spans areas such as mobility, multicast, security, and dynamic network management.

Over the last few years, this work has resulted in multiple designs, prototypes, and implementations developed within national and international research projects in which ANTS actively participates.
